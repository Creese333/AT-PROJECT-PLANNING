9. Project Packet Checklist

In addition to the proposal form, ensure the following are included in your submission packet for NPS review:

Project Type Definitions (2.9)

Facilities
Trail tread, structures, bridges, shelters, and other built infrastructure.
Nat. Res.
Habitat, vegetation, wildlife, invasive species, and other natural resource work.
Cult. Res.
Historic structures, archaeological sites, and other cultural resource work.

Work Type Definitions (2.10)

Cyclic
Cyclic Maintenance — scheduled, recurring upkeep.
New
New Construction — building something not previously present.
I&M
Inventory and Monitoring.
Routine
Routine Maintenance — regular, non-cyclic upkeep.
Replace
Replace an existing asset in kind.
Invasive
Invasive Species Management.
Repair
Repair/Rehabilitation of an existing asset.
Emerg.
Emergency Repair.
Restore
Restoration of a natural or cultural resource.
Remove
Remove/Demolish an asset.
Res. Mgmt.
Resource Management (general).

FMSS Location Types (4.3)

Types
A.T. Treadway, Boundary, Building, Bridge, Campground, Dam, Maintained Landscape, Parking Area, Road, Side Trail, Vista, Water System, Other, N/A.

Priority Level (2.8)

Urgent
Immediate safety, resource, or asset risk that cannot wait for the normal planning cycle.
High
Significant need; should be scheduled in the near term.
Medium
Worthwhile but can be scheduled in the ordinary planning cycle.

Source

APPA
A.T. Planning — Project Proposal Form, April 2021.
